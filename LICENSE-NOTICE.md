# Rights and reuse

No open-source licence is asserted on behalf of the study authors. Repository visibility alone does not grant unrestricted reuse rights. The owner may add an appropriate code/data licence and citation after confirming authorship and permissions.

Included article-level numeric data are aggregate extraction values, not participant-level records. Full-text journal articles, library binaries and unrelated datasets are excluded. R packages remain under their own licences and are installed from their original repositories. Reference PDFs and workbooks are the analysis materials supplied/generated in this project; original journal articles are identified by DOI in `data/study_bibliography.csv`.
